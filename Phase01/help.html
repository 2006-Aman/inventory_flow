Help & Guide

How InventoryIQ works and how to use each screen.

The Reorder Point

Every product is reordered using the classic reorder point formula:

Reorder Point = (Lead Time × Average Daily Demand) + Safety Stock

Lead time is how many days a reorder takes to arrive. Average daily demand is computed from the last 90 days of recorded sales. Safety stock is the buffer you keep for demand spikes or late deliveries.

When current stock drops to the reorder point, the product is flagged Low Stock, below safety stock becomes Critical, and at zero it is Out of Stock.

Demand Forecasting

The forecast uses a 7-day moving average of actual daily sales. Each day's projected demand is the average of the seven days before it. This smooths out daily noise while staying responsive to recent trends.

The Forecast page compares the moving-average forecast against actual sales for the last two weeks, ranks products by expected 7-day demand, and shows how many days of stock remain for each product.

Where Is My Data?

Everything is stored in this browser's LocalStorage. On first open the app seeds itself from the JSON files in data/. Use Settings → Export to back up, and Import to restore on another machine.
